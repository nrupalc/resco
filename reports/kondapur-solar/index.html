Kondapur rooftop solar prospect scan

First-pass apartment-cluster scan for rooftops with no obvious solar-panel arrays visible in Google satellite imagery. The list is designed for field verification, committee outreach, and a second on-ground survey pass.

Area
Kondapur, Hyderabad
Survey date
2026-07-02
Method
Google Maps labels + satellite-tile rooftop review
Output
Height-filtered solar feasibility list
!
Visual rooftop screening cannot prove a site has no solar. It means no clear PV panel grid was visible in the inspected satellite frame. Treat this as a prospect list for salesperson validation, resident contact, drone/terrace access, or a current Google Maps manual recheck.

Sources And Reproduction

Satellite mosaics were generated from Google satellite tiles centered on the coordinates extracted from Google Maps result/place links.

Candidate names and coordinates came from Google Maps search results for apartments in Kondapur Hyderabad.
Each card links back to Google Maps by coordinate for manual verification in the live satellite UI.
Floor and unit-count references are linked directly in the Height Feasibility Filter table. The most useful sources were Housing.com, MagicBricks, 99acres, Greenmark Developers, NoBroker, RoofandFloor, and builder pages.
Snapshot script: uv run --with pillow python reports/kondapur-solar/scripts/generate_satellite_snapshots.py.
