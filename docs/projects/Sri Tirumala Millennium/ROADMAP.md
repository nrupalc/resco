# Roadmap - Sri Tirumala Millennium

## Phase 1 - First approach

Goal: get permission to collect documents and survey the site.

Documents to take:

- Society cover note.
- Working PPA draft, clearly marked draft.
- PPA population checklist.
- Visiting card.
- Bright Roof logo/letterhead if printing.

Do not take:

- Installer quotation.
- Bright Roof capex sheet.
- Payback or investor return calculations.
- Internal partner documents.

Documents to collect:

- 12 months of common-area electricity bills.
- Consumer service number(s).
- Society registration certificate.
- Society bye-laws.
- Current committee list.
- Signatory authority process.
- Full address and contact details.

## Phase 2 - Populate and qualify

Goal: decide whether the site is commercially and legally worth proceeding.

Generate:

- Consumption summary.
- Tariff benchmark calculation.
- Preliminary system sizing note.
- Rooftop/electrical survey request.
- Draft committee or general body resolution.

Populate:

- PPA party details.
- Service numbers and sanctioned load.
- Schedule 1 site details.
- Schedule 3 tariff formula.
- Notice addresses and signatories.

## Phase 3 - Written consent / signature path

Goal: get formal society approval to proceed to installer survey and legal finalisation.

Generate:

- Resolution authorising document sharing and technical survey.
- Updated PPA draft with society-specific fields.
- Legal review question list.

Populate:

- Signatory names and designations.
- Society registration details.
- Survey access contacts.
- Initial commercial decisions: term, security deposit, payment mode, tariff benchmark.

## Phase 4 - After signature / written consent

Goal: get real installation cost and technical scope without turning the society meeting into a capex sale.

Actions:

- Send installers to inspect rooftop, electrical room, cable route, and meter location.
- Get at least two installer quotations.
- Ask installers for system design, BOQ, warranties, monitoring details, and net-metering support.
- Validate whether 18 kWp remains the right size.
- Internally approve Bright Roof capex and investment return.

Association-facing position:

- The society still does not pay installation cost.
- Any installer quote is Bright Roof's funded project cost, not a society payable amount.
- Share only the implementation schedule, safety method, roof protection approach, metering plan, and final tariff formula unless partners deliberately approve more transparency.

## Phase 5 - Final PPA and implementation

Generate:

- Legally reviewed PPA.
- Final general body resolution.
- Site survey report.
- System layout and single-line diagram.
- Installation work schedule.
- Risk assessment and safety plan.
- Net-metering application pack.
- Insurance certificate after procurement.
- COD certificate format.

Populate:

- Final capacity and equipment.
- Final scheduled COD.
- Delivery point and metering details.
- Payment security and bank details.
- O&M response contacts.
