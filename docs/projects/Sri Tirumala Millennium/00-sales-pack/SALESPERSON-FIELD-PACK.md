# Salesperson Field Pack - First Society Approach

## Objective

Get written permission from the Sri Tirumala Millennium society to proceed with document collection, rooftop/electrical survey, and a PPA review path.

The first meeting is not a quotation meeting. It is a trust and permission meeting.

## Customer-facing message

Bright Roof installs the rooftop solar system at its own cost, owns and maintains it, and bills the society only for the solar units consumed or offset. The starting proposal is a 10% saving against the agreed electricity-tariff benchmark. The society does not pay installation cost upfront.

## Questions to ask the committee

| Question | Needed for |
|---|---|
| What is the exact registered name of the society? | PPA party name |
| Is the society registered? Under which statute? | Legal capacity |
| Who can sign long-term utility contracts? | Authority and resolution |
| Can you share the last 12 months of common-area electricity bills? | Tariff benchmark and sizing |
| What are the consumer service numbers? | Net-metering application |
| Has any rooftop solar proposal been discussed before? | Objections and history |
| Are there roof leakage or structural concerns? | Installer quotation and mounting method |
| Who should coordinate access for the roof and electrical room? | Site survey |
| What committee approval process is needed? | Roadmap and resolution |

## Meeting flow

1. Lead with the problem: common-area electricity cost keeps rising.
2. Explain the Bright Roof model: no installation cost to society; pay only for solar units used.
3. State the discount: starting proposal of 10% saving against the agreed electricity-tariff benchmark.
4. Clarify ownership: Bright Roof owns and maintains the system; no automatic handover.
5. Ask for permission to review bills and survey the roof.
6. Leave the cover note, visiting card, and checklist.
7. Schedule the document-collection follow-up before leaving.

## Objections and replies

| Objection | Reply |
|---|---|
| "What is the installation cost?" | Bright Roof funds the installation. The society's commercial decision is the per-unit tariff discount, not the capex. |
| "Will you hand over the system after payback?" | No. Bright Roof keeps ownership and maintenance responsibility for the full term, so the society does not inherit operational risk. |
| "What if the committee changes?" | The PPA is signed by the society, not by individual committee members personally. The legal draft is structured to survive committee changes. |
| "What about roof damage?" | The PPA requires safe installation, insurance, access rules, and responsibility for damage caused by Bright Roof or its installers. |
| "How will billing work?" | A meter and monthly invoice record the solar units. The tariff benchmark will be based on the society's applicable TSSPDCL bill and net-metering treatment. |
