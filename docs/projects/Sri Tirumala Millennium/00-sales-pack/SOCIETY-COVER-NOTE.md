# Bright Roof proposal for Sri Tirumala Millennium

Bright Roof Power Systems is a Hyderabad-based rooftop solar company for apartment societies.

We install, own, operate, and maintain the solar system on the society rooftop. The society does not pay the installation cost upfront. The society pays only for solar units consumed or offset, with a starting proposal of 10% saving against the agreed electricity-tariff benchmark.

## What this means for the society

| Item | Position |
|---|---|
| Upfront installation cost | Nil, funded by Bright Roof |
| Monthly payment | Only for solar units consumed |
| Tariff | Starting proposal: 10% saving against the agreed electricity-tariff benchmark |
| Ownership | Bright Roof owns the system |
| Maintenance | Bright Roof handles operation, maintenance, cleaning, monitoring, and repairs |
| Grid supply | Existing TSSPDCL connection remains for balance electricity |
| Net metering | To be handled through the applicable TSSPDCL process |
| Handover | No automatic handover; Bright Roof remains responsible for the system |

## What Bright Roof needs to review

- Last 12 months of common-area electricity bills.
- Consumer service number(s) and sanctioned load.
- Society registration certificate and bye-laws.
- Current committee office-bearer details.
- Approval process for signing a long-term PPA.
- Rooftop and electrical-room access for a technical survey.

## Immediate next step

If the committee is open to exploring this, Bright Roof will:

1. Review the electricity bills and confirm the tariff benchmark.
2. Inspect the rooftop and electrical connection points.
3. Prepare the final site details for the PPA.
4. Arrange installer quotation and technical design after written consent.
5. Return with a legally reviewed PPA and implementation schedule.

This note is a commercial overview only. The binding terms will be in the signed Power Purchase Agreement.
