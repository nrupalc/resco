# Professional Packaging Guide

## Brand assets to use

Use only the assets copied into `03-brand-assets/`:

| Asset | Use |
|---|---|
| `04-wordmark-horizontal.svg` | Letterheads, email headers, one-page notes |
| `05-wordmark-stacked.svg` | PPA cover page and formal document covers |
| `07-sunburst.svg` | Small mark, seal, favicon, card accent |

Do not use logo explorations, old marks, or mixed styles in the field pack.

## Print rules

- Paper background: cream or white.
- Primary text: Dawn Navy `#0E1E4D`.
- Accent: Roof Amber `#F5A524`, one accent per page.
- Body copy: short sentences, specific numbers, no hype.
- Use `Rs.` in body copy, not currency symbols.
- Keep internal cost and payback data out of association-facing PDFs.

## Before sending externally

- Remove internal-only notes.
- Remove internal drafting comments from the PPA.
- Confirm legal name and address.
- Add version and date.
- Run a legal review before asking for signature.
