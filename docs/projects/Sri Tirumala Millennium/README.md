## Positioning rule

Do not present capex, EPC cost, vendor quotations, or payback calculations to the society during the first approach. The society-facing offer is simple:

> Bright Roof installs the rooftop solar system at its own cost. The society pays only for solar units consumed or offset, with a starting proposal of 10% saving against the agreed electricity-tariff benchmark.

Installer quotes and actual project costs are for Bright Roof internal approval and implementation planning after the society gives written consent to proceed.

## Open decisions before signature

- Confirm the society's exact registered legal name and authority to sign.
- Confirm whether the tariff benchmark is the weighted average effective grid tariff or the final applicable slab rate after net-metering treatment.
- Confirm term length, recommended starting point: 10 years with end-of-term continuation / renewal / purchase options.
- Confirm security deposit and NACH mandate position.
- Get Telangana counsel to review the site-specific PPA before execution.
