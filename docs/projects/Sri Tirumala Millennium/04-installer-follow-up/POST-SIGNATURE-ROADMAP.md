# Post-Signature / Written-Consent Roadmap

This starts only after the society gives written consent to proceed with survey and PPA finalisation.

## Installer quotation workflow

1. Shortlist at least two installers.
2. Share site facts, electricity bills, sanctioned load, and survey access contacts.
3. Conduct rooftop and electrical-room inspection.
4. Confirm usable area, shading, roof condition, mounting type, inverter location, cable route, and meter location.
5. Ask each installer for:
   - BOQ.
   - System capacity recommendation.
   - Module and inverter makes.
   - Monitoring dashboard option.
   - Net-metering scope.
   - Installation schedule.
   - Warranty terms.
   - O&M support terms.
   - Safety and insurance assumptions.
6. Compare quotes internally.
7. Approve capex internally before issuing the final implementation schedule to the society.

## Documents to generate after installer survey

- Site survey report.
- Technical feasibility note.
- Single-line diagram.
- Rooftop layout.
- BOQ.
- Installation schedule.
- Safety and access plan.
- Net-metering application checklist.
- Final PPA schedules.

## What to tell the society

Tell them:

- Bright Roof has completed the technical survey.
- The system remains Bright Roof-funded.
- The society pays no upfront installation cost.
- The implementation plan, safety plan, metering plan, and PPA schedules are ready for review.

Do not tell them:

- Installer margin.
- Bright Roof payback period.
- Partner investment assumptions.
- Quote comparison details.

If the committee asks for project cost, say:

> Bright Roof is funding the system, so the capex is not payable by the society. Your commercial term is the per-unit tariff discount against the agreed grid benchmark. We can share the technical scope, safety method, warranties, and implementation schedule.

## Internal approval gate

Before signing the final PPA, partners should approve:

- Final capex.
- Expected monthly generation.
- Expected monthly billing.
- Payback range.
- O&M cost assumptions.
- Insurance cost.
- Legal review changes.
- Cash contribution split for this project.
