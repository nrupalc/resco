## 2. SCOPE AND PROJECT DESCRIPTION

2.1 The Company shall, at its own cost and risk, design, procure, install, test, commission, own, operate, and maintain the System on the Premises for the Term of this Agreement.

2.2 The System shall have a Contracted Capacity of **18 kWp**, shall conform to the technical specifications in Schedule 2, and shall be installed in accordance with MNRE guidelines, Central Electricity Authority ("**CEA**") regulations on grid-connected solar, TSSPDCL net-metering rules, and all other Applicable Law.

2.3 The Company shall sell, and the Consumer shall purchase, **all** solar energy generated by the System and delivered at the Delivery Point during the Term, on an as-generated basis and on the terms of this Agreement.

2.4 The Consumer shall continue to maintain its existing grid connection with TSSPDCL for the balance of its electricity requirement. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to oblige the Consumer to purchase any minimum quantity of solar energy from the Company, except to the extent of energy actually generated by the System and delivered at the Delivery Point (as-generated, no take-or-pay).

## 3. ROOFTOP LICENCE AND ACCESS

3.1 The Consumer hereby grants to the Company, for the entire Term, an **exclusive, irrevocable, royalty-free licence** to use the rooftop area of the Premises described in Schedule 1 (the "**Licensed Area**") for the purposes of installing, owning, operating, maintaining, repairing, upgrading, and ultimately removing the System.

3.2 The licence granted under Clause 3.1 shall be exclusive in respect of solar photovoltaic generation. The Consumer shall not, during the Term, grant any right to any third party, nor install on its own account, any competing solar generation capacity on the same rooftop that would cause shading, reduce generation, or interfere with the System.

3.3 The Consumer shall provide the Company and its authorised personnel, contractors, and agents with reasonable access to the Licensed Area, riser shafts, cable routes, inverter room, and metering points at all reasonable times upon prior intimation, and at all times without prior intimation in the event of an emergency affecting the safety of persons or property.

3.4 The licence under this Clause 3 is a bare licence to enable the Company to operate the System. It does not create any lease, tenancy, easement, or other interest in the Premises in favour of the Company, and the Consumer's ownership of the Premises is not affected.

3.5 The Company shall not use the Licensed Area for any purpose other than as contemplated in this Agreement, and shall at all times comply with the reasonable house rules of the Consumer relating to safety, noise, and working hours.

5.3 The Tariff applicable during any Renewal Term shall be the Tariff in effect at the end of the Initial Term / previous Renewal Term, subject to a fresh escalation schedule to be mutually agreed in writing not later than six (6) months before the start of such Renewal Term.

## 7. OPERATION, MAINTENANCE AND MONITORING

7.1 Throughout the Term, the Company shall, at its own cost, operate and maintain the System in accordance with prudent industry practice for grid-connected rooftop solar systems, including:

(a) Scheduled preventive maintenance at intervals not less than specified by the equipment manufacturer;

(b) Module cleaning at a frequency sufficient to maintain performance (not less than once per month during dry season);

(c) Remote performance monitoring with 24/7 availability of a monitoring dashboard;

(d) Prompt diagnosis and repair of faults, with response time as set out in Clause 7.3;

(e) Replacement of defective components at the Company's cost throughout the Term.

7.2 **Performance guarantee.** The Company guarantees that the System shall deliver a minimum annual Performance Ratio ("**PR**") of **75%** in Year 1, degrading by no more than **0.7% per year** thereafter, measured as per IEC 61724. If measured PR in any Contract Year falls below the guaranteed PR (after excluding periods of Force Majeure, grid outage not attributable to Company, and Consumer-caused downtime), the Company shall issue the Consumer a credit equal to the shortfall multiplied by the prevailing Tariff. 

7.3 **Response times.** The Company shall acknowledge any fault notified by the Consumer within **four (4) hours** and shall restore normal operation within:

(a) **forty-eight (48) hours** for software / inverter faults; and

(b) **seven (7) days** for faults requiring equipment replacement,

subject to reasonable extensions for Force Majeure and parts lead times beyond the Company's control.

7.4 **Monitoring dashboard.** The Company shall provide the Consumer with read-only login credentials to a web-based monitoring dashboard displaying real-time and historical generation data, system alerts, and downtime log.

## 9. METERING

9.1 The Solar Meter installed by the Company at the Delivery Point shall be the primary meter for the purposes of billing under this Agreement. The Solar Meter shall conform to Central Electricity Authority (Installation and Operation of Meters) Regulations and be of accuracy class 1.0 or better.

9.2 The Solar Meter shall be jointly read on a monthly basis, at a date and time notified by the Company to the Consumer in advance. Meter readings may be taken remotely if the Solar Meter supports remote data acquisition; in that case, the Company shall share a screenshot / downloadable report with the Consumer along with each invoice.

9.3 Meter testing and calibration shall be carried out at the cost of the Company once every **five (5) years**, or earlier on written request of the Consumer (in which case the cost shall be borne by the Consumer if the meter is found to be within tolerance, and by the Company if it is outside tolerance).

9.4 In case of meter failure, readings for the affected period shall be estimated based on the average daily generation of the preceding three (3) months during comparable weather conditions, as measured from the monitoring dashboard.

## 10. TARIFF AND BILLING

10.1 **Tariff.** The Consumer shall pay the Company for all solar energy delivered at the Delivery Point at the following Tariff:

(a) Tariff mechanism: **No installation cost is payable by the Consumer. For each Billing Period, the solar tariff will be set to provide a 10% saving against the applicable benchmark grid tariff for the units consumed or offset through net metering, subject to the final tariff formula in Schedule 3** (exclusive of taxes); 

(b) **Tariff benchmark:** The benchmark grid tariff shall be calculated from the Consumer's applicable TSSPDCL bill and net-metering treatment for the Billing Period. The parties must finalise whether this means (i) the weighted average effective grid tariff for the offset units, or (ii) the final applicable slab rate for those units. In either case, the Consumer-facing starting proposal is a 10% saving against the agreed benchmark.

This is a tariff mechanism only. It does not present project capex, vendor quotation, or installation cost to the Consumer.

10.2 **GST and other taxes.** All amounts payable under this Agreement are exclusive of Goods and Services Tax ("**GST**") and any other taxes, cesses, or duties, which shall be borne by the Consumer at the applicable statutory rate. The Company shall issue a GST-compliant tax invoice for each Billing Period.

10.3 **Invoicing.** Within seven (7) days after the end of each Billing Period, the Company shall issue an invoice to the Consumer setting out (a) opening and closing Solar Meter readings, (b) units of solar energy delivered, (c) Tariff applicable, (d) amount payable, (e) GST, (f) net amount, and (g) any credits or adjustments (including PR shortfall credits under Clause 7.2).

10.4 **Payment due date.** Each invoice shall be payable within **fifteen (15) days** of the invoice date (the "**Due Date**"). 

10.5 **Late payment surcharge.** Amounts unpaid after the Due Date shall bear simple interest at the rate of **1.25% per month (15% per annum)** from the Due Date until payment.

10.6 **Mode of payment.** Payments shall be made by NACH direct-debit from the Consumer's designated bank account in favour of the Company, in accordance with the NACH mandate executed as Schedule 5. In addition, payments may be made by NEFT / RTGS / IMPS to the Company's bank account set out in Schedule 5. No cash payments shall be accepted.

10.7 **Disputed invoices.** The Consumer shall pay all undisputed portions of any invoice by the Due Date and shall, within ten (10) days of receipt of the invoice, give written notice of any disputed portion along with reasonable details. Disputed amounts shall be resolved in accordance with Clause 23.

## 16. FORCE MAJEURE

16.1 A "**Force Majeure Event**" means any event or circumstance beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party which prevents or materially hinders performance of its obligations under this Agreement, including acts of God, earthquake, flood, cyclone, fire not caused by the affected Party, war, riots, civil commotion, strikes of a nationwide or industry-wide nature, epidemic or pandemic, acts of any Governmental Authority (other than TSSPDCL acts contemplated as ordinary grid operations), and prolonged grid outages exceeding seventy-two (72) continuous hours not caused by the affected Party.

16.2 An affected Party shall notify the other Party in writing as soon as reasonably practicable of the occurrence of a Force Majeure Event and its expected duration. The obligations of the affected Party (other than obligations to make payment of amounts already accrued) shall be suspended for the duration of the Force Majeure Event, and the Term shall be extended by an equivalent period.

16.3 If a Force Majeure Event continues for more than **one hundred and eighty (180) continuous days**, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice, with settlement as per Clause 18.

## 18. DEFAULT AND TERMINATION

18.1 **Consumer Default.** Each of the following shall be a "**Consumer Default**":

(a) Failure to pay any undisputed amount within thirty (30) days after the Due Date;

(b) Failure to maintain the Net Metering Agreement or Security Deposit;

(c) Breach of Clauses 3 (Access), 4 (Ownership), or 8.4 (No resale);

(d) Any act, omission, or direction of the Consumer that causes physical damage to the System, excluding fair wear and tear;

(e) Insolvency, winding up, or dissolution of the Consumer.

18.2 **Company Default.** Each of the following shall be a "**Company Default**":

(a) Failure to achieve COD within one hundred eighty (180) days of Scheduled COD, other than by reason of Force Majeure or Consumer Default;

(b) Abandonment of operation of the System for more than sixty (60) continuous days, other than by reason of Force Majeure, Consumer Default, or grid unavailability;

(c) Persistent failure (three or more occurrences in any twelve-month period) to meet response times under Clause 7.3;

(d) Insolvency, winding up, or dissolution of the Company.

18.3 **Cure period.** On occurrence of a Default, the non-defaulting Party shall give the defaulting Party written notice specifying the Default. The defaulting Party shall have **thirty (30) days** from such notice (or, for payment defaults, fifteen (15) days) to cure the Default.

18.4 **Termination.** If the Default is not cured within the cure period, the non-defaulting Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice with immediate effect.

18.5 **Consequences of termination for Consumer Default.** On termination for Consumer Default, the Consumer shall pay the Company, as a genuine pre-estimate of loss, a termination payment equal to: 

> **Termination Payment** = (a) all accrued and unpaid amounts (including late payment surcharge) + (b) the discounted present value of expected Tariff payments for the balance of the Initial Term, using a discount rate of 10% per annum, net of projected operating costs avoided, **or** the depreciated book value of the System multiplied by 1.3, whichever is lower + (c) cost of removal and restoration if the Consumer does not agree to purchase the System under Clause 18.6.

18.6 **Consumer buy-out option on termination for Consumer Default.** On termination for Consumer Default, the Consumer shall have the option, exercisable within thirty (30) days of termination, to purchase the System from the Company at a price equal to **1.3× the depreciated book value** of the System (straight-line over 20 years, zero salvage value). Payment shall be made within sixty (60) days. On payment, title transfers free of encumbrances; the Termination Payment under 18.5(b) shall not apply in addition to the buy-out price.

18.7 **Consequences of termination for Company Default.** On termination for Company Default, the Company shall, at the Consumer's election: (a) remove the System and restore the Licensed Area, at its own cost, within one hundred twenty (120) days; **or** (b) transfer the System to the Consumer for **zero consideration**, free of encumbrances, together with all ongoing manufacturer warranties. The Company shall also refund the Security Deposit and any pre-paid amounts.

18.8 **End-of-term purchase option.** At the end of the Initial Term, the Consumer may request to purchase the System from the Company at the then-applicable fair value / market value to be agreed in writing, taking into account equipment age, condition, remaining warranties, prevailing replacement cost, and any outstanding amounts. On completion of such purchase, this Agreement shall terminate without liability, and the provisions of Clause 19.2 shall apply.
