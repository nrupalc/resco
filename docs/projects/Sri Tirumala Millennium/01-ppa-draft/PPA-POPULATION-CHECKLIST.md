## Commercial decisions

| Decision | Working position |
|---|---|
| Upfront installation cost payable by society | Nil |
| Tariff | 10% saving against agreed electricity-tariff benchmark |
| Capex shown to society | No |
| Term | Open; recommend 10-year initial term with end-of-term continuation / renewal / purchase options |
| Security deposit | Open; recommend 2-3 months estimated billing |
| Payment mode | NACH plus NEFT / RTGS backup |
| Default / disconnection | Needs legal review before final wording |
| Buy-out option | Open; keep out of first sales discussion unless asked |
| End-of-term handover | No automatic handover |

## Legal review items

- Society authority to sign a long-term PPA.
- Stamp duty and registration treatment in Telangana.
- Net-metering paperwork sequence with TSSPDCL.
- Enforceability across future committee changes.
- Disconnection right and consumer-law risk.
- Liability cap, insurance, and roof-damage wording.

## Before external issue

- Replace all `[[...]]` placeholders.
- Remove inline commercial comments.
- Add document date and version.
- Add authorised Bright Roof partner name.
- Attach general body resolution format.
- Attach Schedule 1 site details.
- Attach Schedule 2 technical specifications.
- Attach Schedule 3 tariff formula.
- Attach payment details only after bank account is confirmed.
