# Marketing-imagery prompts (for ChatGPT / Midjourney / Stitch)

Paste these into ChatGPT (DALL·E / GPT-4o image) or Midjourney when we need raster images. Every prompt ends with the same style block so the library feels consistent.

**Common style block — always include:**

> Cinematic, golden-hour light, warm amber sun flare, deep navy sky gradient, terracotta-and-cream Indian architecture, subtle film grain, high-realism photography, shot on 35mm, shallow depth of field where appropriate, no stock-photo look, no generic businesspeople, no exaggerated saturation. Brand palette: navy #0E1E4D, amber #F5A524, terracotta #C75B3C, cream #FAF6EE.

---

## 1. Hero — "Sunrise over the rooftop"

> Wide aerial photograph of a 6-storey Hyderabad apartment complex at sunrise. The rooftop is covered in neatly aligned solar panels. A warm amber sun is rising low on the horizon behind the building. Deep navy sky fading to amber near the horizon. Surrounding terracotta-roofed homes. No people. Clean, aspirational, editorial. *[append common style block]*

## 2. Society committee scene — "People, not stock"

> Documentary-style photograph of three middle-aged Indian men and women in casual business clothes standing on an apartment rooftop, pointing at solar panels and a tablet in one member's hand. Soft morning light. Real, specific, unposed. Background: Hyderabad skyline with neem trees. Shot waist-up. *[append common style block]*

## 3. Engineer on the roof — "Installation"

> Low-angle photograph of an Indian electrician in a navy work shirt and hard hat, tightening a bolt on a solar panel's mounting rail. Golden-hour backlight. Tools in hand. Focus on hands and panel detail, face partially in shadow. Authentic, not posed. *[append common style block]*

## 4. Family on a balcony — "Why we do this"

> Warm candid photograph of an Indian family of four on an apartment balcony in the evening, lamps glowing amber inside, city lights behind. Solar panels visible on the rooftop above. No product placement, no logos. Intimate, honest. *[append common style block]*

## 5. Close-up detail — "The thing itself"

> Extreme close-up photograph of a single solar panel's surface at golden hour, reflecting an amber sunset sky. Drops of water on the glass from a recent cleaning. Macro detail. Calm, meditative. *[append common style block]*

## 6. Society hand-over moment — "Trust"

> Photograph of two hands shaking — one an Indian elderly man (society secretary), the other a younger woman from the Bright Roof team. Ring binder with a PPA document visible on a table. Soft rooftop light. Dignified, warm, not corny. *[append common style block]*

## 7. Dashboard / monitoring illustration (for website)

> Minimal illustration, flat 2D style, of a smartphone screen showing a clean dashboard with a sun icon, a number "1,847 units generated this month", and a simple line chart. Navy background, amber accents, cream text. No gradients, no 3D, no glossy plastic. Style: Stripe docs illustrations crossed with a warm Indian print palette. *[append common style block]*

## 8. Aerial cityscape — "Our rooftop map"

> Top-down aerial photograph of a dense Hyderabad apartment neighbourhood. Some rooftops have solar panels (a few), most don't yet. Morning light. Conveys "there's a lot of sky to claim." *[append common style block]*

---

## Notes for the operator

- Always regenerate 4–6 variations and pick — don't accept the first.
- Ask for "no text, no logos in the image." We composite our wordmark in afterwards.
- Avoid photos of **white Europeans** — all people should read as South Indian. Specify "South Indian" / "Hyderabad" / "Telugu" in the prompt.
- If an output looks like a stock photo (handshake with suits, pointing at laptop, planet-in-hands), reject and regenerate.
- Keep the treatment consistent. One session = one palette and one light direction.
