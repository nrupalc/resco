# Wordmark Options — Pick the Vibe First

Six distinctly different directions for the primary Bright Roof lockup. Each is meant to feel like a **different kind of company** — so pick the *vibe* that fits the Bright Roof you want to build, not the polish. Polish comes after.

Each is rendered as SVG (open in browser / Figma) and a 1600-px PNG (preview, WhatsApp-shareable).

---

## A — Warm Editorial (current primary)

**File:** `../04-wordmark-horizontal.svg`

Serif display (Fraunces-style) + sunburst mark on cream. Pitches like a trusted insurance / wealth / premium-service brand.

**Would suit:** families, committees, banks, long-form PPAs, letterheads. Confident and "safe choice" without being boring.

**Risk:** can read as a bit conservative / legacy.

---

## B — Tech Mono

**File:** `B-techmono.svg` / `.png`

Navy panel, bold geometric sans all-caps, tiny amber disc with a roof-notch, mono tagline with `//` separator.

**Feels like:** Stripe, Linear, Vercel — a dev-tool or SaaS platform.

**Would suit:** monitoring dashboards, mobile app, engineering-led pitch, if we lean into "we run solar like software."

**Risk:** might feel too cold / tech-first to an apartment society committee that's buying trust.

---

## C — Heritage Stamp

**File:** `C-heritage.svg` / `.png`

Framed composition with tall condensed serif, amber corner diamonds, "EST. 2026 · HYDERABAD", italic quote. Reads like a maker's mark.

**Feels like:** Tata Tea / Warby Parker / craft brewery / old-world trust mark.

**Would suit:** letterhead, PPA cover, physical signage, a brand that wants to feel like it has been around for decades even though it hasn't.

**Risk:** not great for app icons / small sizes; a bit ornamental.

---

## D — Indian Modern

**File:** `D-indianmodern.svg` / `.png`

Mandala-inspired radial sun with 24 precise spokes, amber disc, terracotta roof peak inside. Bold humanist sans wordmark with terracotta subtitle.

**Feels like:** Zerodha / Postman / Razorpay — built-in-India, proud of it, geometrically confident.

**Would suit:** Indian-first positioning, billboards, pitch to local committees, branded merchandise. The radial mark is the most ownable / most memorable of the six.

**Risk:** the mandala energy needs to be pulled off with conviction or it can drift toward "temple" associations.

---

## E — Swiss Minimal

**File:** `E-minimal.svg` / `.png`

One amber dot. Thin light-weight sans. A period after "Roof". Vast negative space.

**Feels like:** MUJI / Aesop / Apollo / premium-lifestyle. Confident enough not to show off.

**Would suit:** pitch deck covers, modern site, high-end client segment. Very scaleable — works tiny, works huge.

**Risk:** might feel too "designer" / cold for tier-2-city apartment societies who want to see effort.

---

## F — Typographic Sun

**File:** `F-typographic-sun.svg` / `.png`

"BRIGHT" and "ROOF" stacked on two lines with a giant amber sunburst as the centrepiece between them. Square composition.

**Feels like:** Nordic poster / editorial magazine / hotel brand. The sun becomes the word.

**Would suit:** poster, field signage on a building, social-profile cover photo, T-shirt, tote bag. The most *distinctive* of the six — nobody will confuse it with another brand.

**Risk:** tall/square aspect ratio doesn't work in a letter header; needs a companion horizontal variant for long documents.

---

## How to choose

1. **Forget which one looks "nicer"** — they're all polished. Pick the one whose *vibe* matches the Bright Roof you want customers to feel they're dealing with.
2. Print the PNGs at A4. Tape them on a wall. Walk past them. See which one still feels right after an hour.
3. Ask one non-partner (a potential society secretary, your mum, a CA) — they see it without attachment.
4. Once you pick, I'll build out the full system (stacked lockup, avatar, favicon, letterhead, PPA cover) in that direction and retire the others.
