## Permission Granted

We, the undersigned representative(s) of the above society, permit Bright Roof Power Systems and/or its authorized representatives to carry out the following preliminary review activities:

1. Review electricity bills and related consumption/tariff information shared by the society.
2. Review society registration, office-bearer, and signatory information shared by the society.
3. Visit and inspect the rooftop, electrical room, meter area, cable route, and related common areas by prior appointment.
4. Take site photographs and notes required for technical and commercial feasibility review.
5. Prepare a preliminary proposal or draft documentation for committee review.

## Clarifications

- This permission is only for preliminary review and survey.
- This permission does not create an obligation for the society to sign a final agreement.
- Bright Roof will not begin installation work based only on this consent.
- Final commercial, legal, technical, payment, and installation terms will be governed by separately approved and signed documents.
- Site visits will be coordinated with the society's nominated representative.
