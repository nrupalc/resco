# Committee Pitch Deck Outline

**Audience:** Apartment society committee  
**Format:** 6-slide pitch deck  
**Status:** Draft content

## Slide 1: Bright Roof Power Systems

**Headline:** Rooftop solar for apartment societies, without upfront installation cost

**Supporting copy:**  
Bright Roof installs, owns, operates, and maintains rooftop solar systems. Your society pays only for solar electricity under the signed agreement, with a starting proposal of 10% saving against the agreed electricity-tariff benchmark.

**Footer:** Solar that stays.

## Slide 2: The Problem

**Headline:** Common-area electricity cost keeps rising

**Points:**

- Lifts, pumps, lighting, clubhouse, STP, and other common loads add up every month.
- Many societies want solar but do not want to collect lakhs from residents.
- Committees do not want to carry long-term repair, replacement, and maintenance risk.

## Slide 3: The Bright Roof Model

**Headline:** We fund and maintain the system. The society gets lower power cost.

**Points:**

- No upfront installation cost to the society.
- Bright Roof owns and maintains the rooftop solar system.
- Society pays for solar electricity under the agreement.
- Existing grid supply remains for balance electricity.
- Starting proposal: 10% saving against agreed benchmark.

## Slide 4: What The Society Needs To Provide

**Headline:** First we verify fit. Then we propose.

**Points:**

- 12 months common-area electricity bills.
- Consumer service number and sanctioned load details.
- Society registration and office-bearer information.
- Rooftop and electrical-room access for survey.
- Approval/signatory process.

## Slide 5: Process And Timeline

**Headline:** Clear steps before any final commitment
