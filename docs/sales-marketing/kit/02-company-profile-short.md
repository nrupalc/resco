## What Bright Roof Does

Bright Roof Power Systems is a Hyderabad-based rooftop solar company focused on apartment societies.

We install, own, operate, and maintain rooftop solar systems. The society does not pay the installation cost upfront. The society pays only for solar electricity consumed or offset under the signed agreement, with a starting proposal of 10% saving against the agreed electricity-tariff benchmark.

## Who We Serve

Bright Roof is designed for apartment societies with meaningful common-area electricity consumption, such as:

- Lifts
- Pumps
- Common lighting
- Clubhouse
- STP
- Security and maintenance loads
- Other shared facilities

The model is best suited to societies where monthly common-area electricity bills are large enough for solar savings to matter.

## How The Process Works

| Step | What Happens |
|---|---|
| 1. Bill review | Bright Roof reviews 12 months of electricity bills and tariff details. |
| 2. Site survey | Rooftop, electrical room, meter location, and cable route are inspected. |
| 3. Proposal | Bright Roof prepares a society-specific commercial and technical proposal. |
| 4. Agreement | Final terms are documented in a Power Purchase Agreement. |
| 5. Installation | Bright Roof coordinates installation, safety, and approvals. |
| 6. Operation | Bright Roof monitors, maintains, cleans, and supports the system. |

## What The Society Pays

The society does not pay upfront installation cost.

The society pays for solar electricity under the signed agreement. The starting proposal is a 10% saving against the agreed electricity-tariff benchmark, subject to bill review, technical feasibility, and final agreement terms.

## First Step For A Society

The first step is permission to review bills and inspect the rooftop/electrical room.

Documents usually needed:

- Last 12 months of common-area electricity bills.
- Consumer service number(s).
- Sanctioned load details if available.
- Society registration certificate and bye-laws.
- Current office-bearer list.
- Approval/signatory process.

This profile is a general overview. Final project terms will be governed by site-specific documents and the signed Power Purchase Agreement.
