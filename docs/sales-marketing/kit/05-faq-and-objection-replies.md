# FAQ And Objection Replies

**Audience:** Internal sales team  
**Use:** Field reference for Srinivas, Vijay, and Kalyan  
**Status:** Draft

## Core Rule

Keep answers short. Do not over-explain. If a question affects price, contract, liability, or technical design, say Bright Roof will confirm after bill review and survey.

## Commercial Questions

### Is installation really free?

There is no upfront installation payment from the society. Bright Roof funds, owns, operates, and maintains the rooftop solar system. The society pays only for solar electricity under the signed agreement.

### What saving do we get?

The starting proposal is 10% saving against the agreed electricity-tariff benchmark. Bright Roof confirms the exact benchmark and proposal after reviewing bills and site feasibility.

### Can you give more than 10%?

Possibly, but we should not commit before review. Bright Roof can check whether a higher saving is possible after seeing the bills, rooftop feasibility, and expected system size.

### What is the installation cost?

Bright Roof funds the system. The society's decision is about the tariff saving and agreement terms, not paying the installation cost upfront.

### Why should we not buy the system ourselves?

The society can buy a system directly if it wants to collect capex and manage maintenance. Bright Roof is for societies that want solar savings without upfront collection, technical ownership, and long-term maintenance burden.

## Ownership And Maintenance

### Who owns the system?

Bright Roof owns the system during the agreement period.

### Who maintains it?

Bright Roof handles operation, cleaning, monitoring, repair coordination, and maintenance obligations as defined in the agreement.

### Will Bright Roof hand over the system after payback?

No automatic handover is part of the standard Bright Roof model. Bright Roof remains responsible for the system, so the society does not inherit operational risk.

## Technical Questions

### What system size will be installed?

That depends on electricity consumption, sanctioned load, rooftop space, electrical connection, and survey results. We do not finalize size before review.

### How much generation can we expect?

Bright Roof can estimate generation after checking rooftop space, shading, orientation, system size, and installer design.

### Will the grid connection continue?

Yes. The existing grid connection remains for balance electricity and backup as applicable.

### What about net metering?

Bright Roof will review the applicable process and technical requirements during proposal preparation. Net-metering treatment depends on the connection, local process, and project design.

## Roof And Safety

### What if the roof is damaged?

The final agreement and installation method will address safe installation, access rules, roof protection, insurance, and responsibility for damage caused by Bright Roof or its installers.

### Will there be drilling?

The mounting method is decided after rooftop survey and installer design. If the society has leakage concerns, those must be reviewed before final proposal.

### Who handles safety during installation?

Bright Roof and its installer will follow an agreed work plan, safety requirements, and access process before work begins.

## Legal And Approval

### Can the current committee sign this?

That depends on the society's registration, bye-laws, and approval process. Bright Roof needs to review the authorized signatory and resolution process before final agreement.

### What if the committee changes?

The agreement is with the society, not individual committee members personally. The legal draft should be structured to continue beyond committee changes.

### Are we signing anything today?

No final PPA is required in the first meeting. The first step is permission for bill review and rooftop/electrical survey.

## Escalate Immediately If Asked

Escalate to Nrupal before answering in writing if the society asks:

- Exact tariff commitment.
- Discount above 10%.
- Final system capacity.
- Contract duration changes.
- Handover after payback.
- Roof damage liability wording.
- Payment default consequences.
- Legal enforceability.
- Net-metering guarantee.
- Installer brand guarantee.
