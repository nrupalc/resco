# Team Field Roles

## Role Summary

| Person | Primary Function | Best Used For | Escalates To Nrupal For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Srinivas | Technical credibility and installer/vendor confidence. | Technical questions, rooftop feasibility, installer coordination, electrical-department/vendor language. | Proposal formatting, calculators, formal documents, website updates. |
| Vijay | Warm local access and trust-building. | Apartment contacts, society conversations, document collection, first pilot-style discussions. | Society-specific pack, bill analysis, PPA population, follow-up material. |
| Kalyan | Lead generation and logistics. | Building lead list, finding contact persons, arranging meetings, follow-up discipline. | Scripts, tracker updates, locality-specific material, qualification help. |
| Nrupal | Central sales support and systems. | Documents, website, calculators, proposal templates, brand/marketing assets, tracking process. | Needs timely field data from all three. |

## Srinivas

### Primary Mission

Make Bright Roof feel technically credible without turning the first meeting into an engineering session.

### Responsibilities

- Answer high-level technical questions from committees.
- Explain rooftop survey requirements.
- Coordinate installer/vendor conversations after society permission.
- Review whether a lead looks technically realistic.
- Help define survey photo requirements.
- Help validate inverter, panel, net-metering, and monitoring assumptions.

### Should Carry

- Visiting card.
- One-page leaflet.
- First-meeting checklist.
- Technical FAQ.
- Site survey checklist.

### Should Avoid Carrying

- Vendor quotation.
- Cost-per-kW details.
- Internal payback or investor return sheets.
- Alternative vendor comparison sheets.

### Escalation Trigger

Escalate to Nrupal when:

- A society shares electricity bills.
- A committee asks for written proposal material.
- A technical question affects pricing or contract obligations.
- A society asks for a stronger discount.

## Vijay

### Primary Mission

Use local trust and apartment-network access to get the first serious committee conversation.

### Responsibilities

- Identify apartment societies through personal/local network.
- Get warm introductions to committee members.
- Collect bills and society documents after permission.
- Keep follow-up moving.
- Help test the first complete sales process.

### Should Carry

- Visiting card.
- One-page leaflet.
- Company profile.
- Document request checklist.
- Consent/sign-off form.

### Should Avoid Carrying

- Installer quote.
- Partner/equity notes.
- Internal commercial model.
- Raw PPA unless the committee is ready for legal review.

### Escalation Trigger

Escalate to Nrupal when:

- A society asks for anything in writing.
- A recent electricity bill is available.
- A committee meeting is scheduled.
- A society asks, "How much will we save?"

## Kalyan

### Primary Mission

Build and maintain the lead pipeline.

### Responsibilities

- Create the first 30-society target list.
- Find contact persons and warm-intro routes.
- Maintain lead status and follow-up dates.
- Arrange first calls and meetings.
- Track objections and reasons for rejection.

### Should Carry

- Visiting card.
- One-page leaflet.
- WhatsApp intro script.
- Lead qualification checklist.

### Should Avoid Carrying

- Draft PPA.
- Technical design sheets.
- Vendor details.
- Pricing beyond the starting 10% saving message.

### Escalation Trigger

Escalate to Nrupal when:

- A lead has a real contact person.
- Monthly common-area bill appears above Rs. 40,000.
- The committee wants a meeting.
- A society asks for brochure, profile, or website link.

## Nrupal Support Responsibilities

Nrupal should provide:

- Master lead tracker.
- Sales scripts.
- Leaflets, brochures, cards, and PDF packs.
- Website updates.
- QR codes.
- Bill-analysis templates.
- Proposal calculator.
- Society-specific proposal notes.
- PPA population support.
- Central document quality control.

Nrupal needs from the field team:

- Society name.
- Area/locality.
- Contact person and role.
- Approximate flat count.
- Approximate monthly common-area bill.
- Last 12 months of electricity bills when available.
- Photos of roof, electrical room, meter, cable routes, and common areas.
- Objections heard in the meeting.
- Next follow-up date.
