# Sales Kit Roadmap

## Purpose

Create a repeatable sales and marketing system for finding Bright Roof's first apartment society client in Hyderabad.

The system must help Srinivas, Vijay, and Kalyan:

- Find suitable societies.
- Get warm introductions.
- Explain the Bright Roof model simply.
- Collect the right documents.
- Avoid overcommitting on pricing or technical details.
- Escalate qualified opportunities to Nrupal for proposal, calculator, website, and document support.

## Operating Principle

Every sales asset should help move a society from one clear stage to the next.

The sales team should not carry internal investor material, vendor cost sheets, payback models, equity notes, or raw installation quotations into first meetings.

## Sales Funnel

| Stage | Meaning | Owner | Exit Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead identified | Society name and area known. | Srinivas / Vijay / Kalyan | Contact person identified. |
| Warm intro | Someone can introduce Bright Roof to a resident, secretary, president, or facility manager. | Srinivas / Vijay / Kalyan | First call or visit scheduled. |
| First conversation | Basic model explained. | Salesperson | Society shows interest or rejects. |
| Committee meeting | More formal conversation with decision makers. | Salesperson + Srinivas if technical | Committee asks for next step. |
| Document request | Bills and society records requested. | Salesperson | Written/email/WhatsApp approval received. |
| Bill review | Nrupal reviews bills and estimates benchmark. | Nrupal | Site appears viable or non-viable. |
| Survey permission | Rooftop/electrical access permitted. | Salesperson | Survey date agreed. |
| Proposal preparation | Society-specific proposal prepared. | Nrupal + Srinivas | Proposal pack ready. |
| PPA review | Draft agreement reviewed by committee/legal. | Nrupal + local legal/CA | Objections tracked. |
| Society approval | Resolution or authorized approval received. | Salesperson | Final execution path open. |

## Do Not Create Yet

These are useful later but not needed before first client traction.

- Full customer app.
- Automated billing product.
- Complex CRM.
- Paid ads.
- Multi-language campaign suite.
- Large brochure catalog.
- Merchandise.

## Decision Rules

- Start customer conversations at 10% saving.
- Do not commit above 10% without partner approval and bill review.
- Do not promise system size before survey.
- Do not promise exact monthly savings before bill review.
- Do not discuss internal installation cost unless partners approve case-by-case.
- Do not present vendor quote as society payable amount.
