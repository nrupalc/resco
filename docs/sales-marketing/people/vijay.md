# Vijay Field Kit

## Role

Warm local access, first trust conversation, document collection, and follow-up.

Vijay is likely the strongest person for opening apartment-society doors because of local relationships and the Sri Tirumala Millennium context.

## Primary Sales Mission

Get the first real society opportunity from conversation to document sharing.

## Best Lead Sources

- Own apartment and nearby apartment network.
- Friends and family in Hyderabad apartments.
- Society committee contacts.
- Facility managers and apartment maintenance offices.
- Residents frustrated with common-area electricity bills.

## What Vijay Should Say

Bright Roof is not asking the society to pay for the solar system upfront. Bright Roof funds, owns, and maintains it. The society pays only for solar electricity under the agreement, starting with a 10% saving against the agreed electricity-tariff benchmark. The first step is only bill review and rooftop survey permission.

## What Vijay Should Avoid Saying

- Do not say the society will definitely save a fixed rupee amount before bill review.
- Do not say the final agreement is ready for signature in the first meeting.
- Do not share internal payback or investor return calculations.
- Do not mention partner ownership/equity.
- Do not show installer quote as if it is a customer-facing price.

## Carry Pack

- Visiting card.
- One-page leaflet.
- Company profile.
- Document request checklist.
- Bill-sharing and survey consent form.
- Meeting notes template.

## Escalate To Nrupal When

- A society asks for a brochure, PDF, or website link.
- A bill is received.
- Committee meeting is scheduled.
- A society wants a proposal.
- Follow-up needs formal wording.

## Vijay WhatsApp Intro

Hi [Name], this is Vijay from Bright Roof Power Systems. We are working with apartment societies in Hyderabad on rooftop solar where the society does not pay the installation cost upfront.

Bright Roof installs, owns, and maintains the system. The society pays only for solar electricity under the agreement, starting with a 10% saving against the agreed electricity-tariff benchmark.

Would your committee be open to a short discussion for [Society Name]? The first step is only bill review and rooftop survey permission, not final agreement signing.

## Vijay First-Meeting Focus

- Build trust and keep the conversation simple.
- Ask for one recent bill first if 12 months are not immediately available.
- Get the right committee contact and approval path.
- Secure permission for bill review and rooftop/electrical survey.
- Send all documents to Nrupal before promising proposal numbers.
