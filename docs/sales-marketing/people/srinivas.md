# Srinivas Field Kit

## Role

Technical credibility, vendor confidence, and feasibility judgment.

Srinivas should help committees feel that Bright Roof understands rooftop solar, electrical constraints, installation safety, and long-term maintenance.

## Primary Sales Mission

Support serious conversations where technical trust matters, especially after Vijay or Kalyan has opened the door.

## Best Lead Sources

- Installer/vendor network.
- Electrical department or approval-process contacts.
- Apartment associations where technical concerns are already known.
- Facility managers and maintenance supervisors.
- Societies that have already considered solar but did not proceed.

## What Srinivas Should Say

Bright Roof will not finalize system size or installation method without checking the rooftop, electrical room, sanctioned load, bill history, and net-metering path. The first step is a proper review, not a rushed quote.

## What Srinivas Should Avoid Saying

- Do not promise exact kW size before survey.
- Do not promise exact generation before design.
- Do not quote vendor pricing in society meetings.
- Do not commit above 10% saving without partner approval.
- Do not make verbal assurances about roof damage beyond the approved wording.

## Carry Pack

- Visiting card.
- One-page leaflet.
- Technical FAQ.
- Site survey checklist.
- Meeting notes template.

## Escalate To Nrupal When

- Committee asks for written proposal.
- Bills are shared.
- Society wants a technical document.
- Installer/vendor input needs to be converted into customer-facing language.
- Any number needs to go into writing.
