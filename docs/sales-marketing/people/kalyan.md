# Kalyan Field Kit

## Role

Lead generation, contact discovery, meeting logistics, and follow-up tracking.

Kalyan's main value is building the top of the funnel and keeping the sales process moving.

## Primary Sales Mission

Create and maintain a pipeline of qualified Hyderabad apartment-society leads.

## Best Lead Sources

- Google Maps apartment searches.
- Personal network.
- Locality-based resident contacts.
- Security desks and maintenance offices.
- Facility managers.
- Electricians, plumbers, lift technicians, and service vendors.

## What Kalyan Should Say

Bright Roof helps apartment societies reduce common-area electricity bills with no upfront solar installation cost. We install and maintain the system, and the society pays only for solar electricity under the agreement. If the committee is open, we can first review bills and inspect the rooftop.

## What Kalyan Should Avoid Saying

- Do not get into deep technical design.
- Do not promise exact savings.
- Do not discuss vendor cost.
- Do not commit to dates, pricing, or capacity without Srinivas/Nrupal review.
- Do not send draft PPA unless instructed.

## Carry Pack

- Visiting card.
- One-page leaflet.
- WhatsApp intro script.
- Lead qualification checklist.
- Meeting notes template.

## Escalate To Nrupal When

- A contact person is found.
- A society asks for PDF material.
- Monthly bill looks large enough.
- A committee meeting may be possible.
- The lead tracker needs updated statuses or prioritization.

## Kalyan WhatsApp Intro

Hi [Name], this is Kalyan from Bright Roof Power Systems. We help apartment societies reduce common-area electricity bills using rooftop solar, without the society paying upfront installation cost.

We first check whether the society is suitable by reviewing electricity bills and rooftop access. If it looks relevant for [Society Name], can I connect you with the Bright Roof team for a short discussion?

## Kalyan First-Meeting Focus

- Find the correct committee or facility contact.
- Confirm approximate monthly common-area bill.
- Confirm whether a warm introduction is possible.
- Add every lead to the tracker with next follow-up date.
- Do not go deep into technical or legal details.
