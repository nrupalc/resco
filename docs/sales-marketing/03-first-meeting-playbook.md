# First Meeting Playbook

## Meeting Objective

The first meeting is a permission and trust meeting.

The goal is to secure permission for:

1. Sharing 12 months of common-area electricity bills.
2. Sharing society registration and office-bearer details.
3. Rooftop and electrical-room access for survey.
4. Preparing a populated proposal or PPA draft after data review.

The goal is not to close the final agreement in the first conversation.

## Customer-Facing Opening

Bright Roof installs, owns, operates, and maintains rooftop solar systems for apartment societies. The society does not pay upfront installation cost. The society pays only for solar electricity consumed or offset under the agreement, with a starting proposal of 10% saving against the agreed electricity-tariff benchmark.

## 30-Second Pitch

Bright Roof helps apartment societies reduce common-area electricity bills without paying for the solar system upfront. We install and maintain the rooftop solar system at our cost. The society pays only for solar units used under the agreement, starting with a 10% saving against the agreed electricity tariff. If the committee is open to it, the first step is simply to review the last 12 months of bills and inspect the rooftop.

## 2-Minute Pitch

Most apartment societies want solar but do not want to collect lakhs of rupees from residents, manage installation risk, or handle maintenance for the next 20 years. Bright Roof solves that through a rooftop solar PPA model.

We fund the system, install it, own it, operate it, and maintain it. The society keeps its normal grid connection and pays Bright Roof only for the solar electricity consumed or offset under the signed agreement. The starting proposal is a 10% saving against the agreed electricity-tariff benchmark.

At this stage we are not asking the society to sign the final PPA. We only need permission to review the electricity bills, understand the rooftop and electrical room, and come back with a proper site-specific proposal.

## What To Carry

- Visiting card.
- One-page society leaflet.
- Company profile or short cover note.
- Document request checklist.
- Bill-sharing and survey consent form.
- Notebook or meeting notes template.

## Optional To Carry

- Draft PPA review copy, only if the committee wants to understand legal structure.
- Six-slide pitch deck, only for planned committee meetings.

## Do Not Carry

- Vendor quotation.
- Internal capex sheet.
- Investor return model.
- Partner equity details.
- Raw installer comparison.
- Pricing commitments above 10%.

## Questions To Ask

| Question | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| What is the exact registered name of the society? | Needed for proposal and PPA. |
| Who are the current office bearers? | Helps identify signatories and follow-up owner. |
| Who can approve document sharing and rooftop survey? | First permission step. |
| What is the approximate monthly common-area electricity bill? | Early qualification. |
| Can the society share the last 12 months of bills? | Required for benchmark and sizing. |
| What are the consumer service numbers and sanctioned load? | Needed for electrical review and net metering. |
| Has rooftop solar been discussed earlier? | Surfaces objections and history. |
| Are there roof leakage or structural concerns? | Affects feasibility and trust. |
| Who controls roof and electrical room access? | Needed for survey scheduling. |
| What is the committee approval process? | Determines sales timeline. |

## Objections And Replies

| Objection | Suggested Reply |
|---|---|
| What is the installation cost? | Bright Roof funds the installation. The society's decision is about the per-unit saving and agreement terms, not paying the capex. |
| Is installation really free? | There is no upfront installation payment from the society. The society pays only for solar electricity under the signed agreement. |
| Who owns the system? | Bright Roof owns and maintains the system during the agreement period. |
| Will you hand it over after payback? | No automatic handover. Bright Roof remains responsible, so the society does not inherit maintenance and replacement risk. |
| What if the committee changes? | The agreement is with the society, not with individual committee members personally. |
| What about roof damage? | The final agreement and installation plan will cover safe mounting, access, insurance, and responsibility for damage caused by Bright Roof or its installers. |
| How will billing work? | Billing will be based on metered solar generation/consumption or offset treatment as defined in the agreement. We confirm the details after bill review and technical survey. |
| Can you give more than 10% saving? | The starting proposal is 10%. We can review whether a better saving is possible after seeing the bills and site feasibility, but we should not commit before that. |

## Closing Ask

If the committee is open to exploring this, the useful next step is written permission to review bills and inspect the rooftop/electrical room. After that, Bright Roof can prepare a proper site-specific proposal.

## Follow-Up Rule

- Same day: send thank-you message and list of documents needed.
- After 2 days: follow up for bills/documents.
- After 7 days: ask for survey date or decision.
- After 14 days: mark as stalled unless there is a clear next action.
