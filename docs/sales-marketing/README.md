## Naming Rule

Use **Bright Roof Power Systems** or **Bright Roof** externally.

Use **RESCO** only as the internal business model term. Do not use RESCO as the company name in visiting cards, brochures, proposal covers, or website copy.

## Core Customer Message

Bright Roof installs, owns, operates, and maintains rooftop solar systems for apartment societies. The society pays no upfront installation cost. The society pays only for solar electricity consumed or offset under the agreement, with a starting proposal of 10% saving against the agreed electricity-tariff benchmark.
