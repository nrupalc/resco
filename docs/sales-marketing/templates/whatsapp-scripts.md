# WhatsApp Scripts

These are draft scripts for first contact and follow-up. Keep messages short and plain.

## Warm Intro Message

Hi [Name], this is [Salesperson] from Bright Roof Power Systems. We help apartment societies reduce common-area electricity bills through rooftop solar with no upfront installation cost to the society.

Bright Roof installs, owns, and maintains the system. The society pays only for solar electricity under the agreement, starting with a 10% saving against the agreed electricity-tariff benchmark.

Would it be okay to speak for 10 minutes and see if this is relevant for [Society Name]?

## After First Call

Thank you for speaking today. As discussed, the useful first step is only a basic review, not a final commitment.

Could you please help with:

1. Last 12 months of common-area electricity bills.
2. Consumer service number / sanctioned load details if available.
3. Society registration or office-bearer details if the committee wants to proceed.
4. Permission for rooftop and electrical-room inspection after initial bill review.

Once we review this, Bright Roof can come back with a proper site-specific proposal.

## Committee Meeting Request

Hi [Name], based on our discussion, it would be useful to explain the Bright Roof model to the committee directly.

The meeting can be short: 20-30 minutes. We will cover:

- No upfront installation cost to the society.
- How the society gets savings.
- What documents are needed.
- What rooftop/electrical survey is required.
- What the next step would be.

Please let us know a convenient time.

## Two-Day Follow-Up

Hi [Name], just following up on the electricity bills/document checklist for [Society Name]. Once we receive the bills, we can check whether the rooftop solar PPA model is commercially suitable before asking for any further time from the committee.

## Seven-Day Follow-Up

Hi [Name], checking whether the committee would like Bright Roof to continue the review for [Society Name]. If yes, the next useful step is sharing the bills or allowing us to schedule the rooftop/electrical-room inspection.

## Stalled Lead Message

Hi [Name], we have not heard back, so we will pause the Bright Roof review for now. If the committee wants to revisit rooftop solar savings later, please message or call us anytime.
