## 2. SCOPE

2.1 The Company shall, at its own cost and risk, design, procure, install, test, commission, own, operate and maintain the System on the Premises for the Term of this Agreement.

2.2 The Company shall sell, and the Consumer shall purchase, all solar energy generated by the System and delivered at the Delivery Point during the Term, on an as-generated basis, at the Tariff and on the terms set out in this Agreement.

2.3 The Consumer shall continue to maintain its existing grid connection with TSSPDCL for the balance of its electricity requirement. Nothing in this Agreement shall oblige the Consumer to purchase any minimum quantity of solar energy, except to the extent of energy actually generated by the System and delivered at the Delivery Point.

---

## 3. LICENCE OF ROOFTOP

3.1 The Consumer hereby grants to the Company, for the entire Term, an exclusive, irrevocable and royalty-free licence to use the rooftop area of the Premises described in Schedule 1 (the **"Licensed Area"**) for the purposes of installing, owning, operating, maintaining, repairing, upgrading and ultimately removing the System.

3.2 The licence under Clause 3.1 shall be exclusive in respect of solar photovoltaic generation. The Consumer shall not, during the Term, permit any third party or itself to install any competing solar generation capacity on the rooftop that would cause shading of, reduce generation from, or interfere with the System.

3.3 The Consumer shall provide the Company, its personnel, contractors and agents with reasonable access to the Licensed Area, cable routes, inverter room and metering points at all reasonable times upon prior intimation, and at all times without prior intimation in the event of an emergency affecting the safety of persons or property.

3.4 The licence granted under this Clause 3 is a bare licence to enable the Company to operate the System, and does not create any lease, tenancy, easement or other proprietary interest in the Premises in favour of the Company.

---

## 4. OWNERSHIP OF THE SYSTEM

4.1 The System is, and shall at all times during and after the Term remain, the sole and exclusive property of the Company. The Parties acknowledge that the System is not a fixture, attachment or improvement to the Premises, and that notwithstanding the physical affixation of any component to the Premises, title to all components of the System shall vest solely in the Company.

4.2 The Consumer shall not, and shall procure that no member or occupant of the Premises shall, tamper with, modify, sell, charge, mortgage, encumber or otherwise deal with the System or any component thereof.

4.3 For the avoidance of doubt, there shall be no automatic transfer of the System to the Consumer upon expiry or earlier termination of this Agreement. End-of-term arrangements are governed exclusively by Clause 19.

---

## 5. TERM

5.1 This Agreement shall come into force on the Effective Date and shall remain in force for a period of **[___] years from the COD** (the **"Initial Term"**), unless terminated earlier in accordance with its terms.

5.2 The Initial Term shall automatically extend for successive periods of five (5) years each (each a **"Renewal Term"**, and together with the Initial Term, the **"Term"**), unless either Party gives the other Party written notice of non-renewal at least twelve (12) months before the end of the Initial Term or the then-current Renewal Term.

5.3 The Tariff applicable during any Renewal Term shall be mutually agreed in writing by the Parties not later than six (6) months before the commencement of such Renewal Term.

---

## 6. DESIGN, INSTALLATION AND COMMISSIONING

6.1 The Company shall complete the design, installation, testing and commissioning of the System on or before the Scheduled COD, subject to Force Majeure and delays caused by the Consumer or any Governmental Authority.

6.2 The Company shall, at its own cost:

(a) prepare and submit all drawings, applications and approvals required from TSSPDCL, the Chief Electrical Inspectorate, and any other authority;

(b) procure all equipment in accordance with Schedule 2;

(c) carry out civil, electrical and structural works in accordance with Applicable Law and prudent industry practice; and

(d) install revenue-grade metering as required under the Net Metering Agreement, together with a sub-meter at the Delivery Point (the **"Solar Meter"**) of accuracy class 1.0 or better.

6.3 The Consumer shall, at its own cost:

(a) provide clear, dry and structurally sound access to the Licensed Area;

(b) execute and maintain the Net Metering Agreement with TSSPDCL in its own name;

(c) obtain all internal approvals required under its bye-laws for this Agreement and the works contemplated herein; and

(d) provide temporary electricity and water at actual cost during the installation period.

---

## 7. OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE

7.1 Throughout the Term, the Company shall, at its own cost, operate and maintain the System in accordance with prudent industry practice, including scheduled preventive maintenance, module cleaning, remote performance monitoring and prompt repair of faults.

7.2 The Company shall acknowledge any fault notified by the Consumer within four (4) hours of such notification, and shall use reasonable endeavours to restore normal operation within forty-eight (48) hours for software or inverter faults, and within seven (7) days for faults requiring equipment replacement.

7.3 The Company shall provide the Consumer with read-only access to a monitoring dashboard displaying real-time and historical generation data.

7.4 The Company warrants that the System shall deliver a minimum annual Performance Ratio of **[___]%** in the first Contract Year, degrading by no more than **[___]%** per annum thereafter, measured in accordance with IEC 61724. If the measured Performance Ratio in any Contract Year falls below the warranted value (after excluding periods of Force Majeure, grid unavailability and Consumer-caused downtime), the Company shall credit the Consumer, in the succeeding Billing Period, with an amount equal to the shortfall in units multiplied by the prevailing Tariff.

---

## 8. SALE AND PURCHASE OF SOLAR ENERGY

8.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Company shall sell and deliver, and the Consumer shall purchase and take delivery of, all solar energy generated by the System and delivered at the Delivery Point during the Term.

8.2 Title to and risk in the solar energy shall pass from the Company to the Consumer at the Delivery Point.

8.3 Any financial benefit accruing on account of solar energy exported to the TSSPDCL grid under the Net Metering Agreement shall be dealt with as set out in Schedule 3.

8.4 The Consumer shall not resell or redirect the solar energy supplied under this Agreement to any third party without the prior written consent of the Company.

---

## 9. METERING

9.1 The Solar Meter installed at the Delivery Point shall be the primary meter for the purposes of billing under this Agreement.

9.2 The Solar Meter shall be read on a monthly basis, at a date and time notified by the Company in advance. Where the Solar Meter supports remote data acquisition, readings may be taken remotely, and a downloadable report shall be shared with the Consumer along with each invoice.

9.3 The Solar Meter shall be tested and calibrated at the cost of the Company once every five (5) years, or earlier on the written request of the Consumer, in which case the cost shall be borne by the Consumer if the meter is found to be within tolerance, and by the Company if it is found to be outside tolerance.

9.4 In the event of meter failure, readings for the affected period shall be estimated on the basis of the average daily generation of the preceding three (3) months, as recorded by the monitoring system.

---

## 10. TARIFF AND BILLING

10.1 The Consumer shall pay the Company for all solar energy delivered at the Delivery Point at a base Tariff of **Rs. [___] per kWh** (exclusive of applicable taxes) for the first Contract Year, subject to escalation as set out in Schedule 3.

10.2 All amounts payable under this Agreement are exclusive of Goods and Services Tax and any other taxes, cesses or duties, which shall be borne by the Consumer at the applicable statutory rate. The Company shall issue a tax invoice for each Billing Period.

10.3 Within seven (7) days after the end of each Billing Period, the Company shall issue an invoice to the Consumer setting out the opening and closing Solar Meter readings, the units of solar energy supplied, the applicable Tariff, any credits or adjustments, the taxes payable and the net amount due.

10.4 Each invoice shall be payable within **[fifteen (15) / thirty (30)]** days of the invoice date (the **"Due Date"**). Amounts remaining unpaid after the Due Date shall bear simple interest at the rate of **1.25% per month** from the Due Date until payment in full.

10.5 Payments shall be made by NACH direct debit pursuant to the mandate set out in Schedule 5, or by NEFT / RTGS / IMPS to the Company's bank account as notified from time to time. No cash payments shall be accepted.

10.6 The Consumer shall pay all undisputed portions of any invoice by the Due Date. The Consumer shall notify the Company of any disputed portion, with reasonable particulars, within ten (10) days of receipt of the invoice. Disputed amounts shall be resolved in accordance with Clause 23.

## 11. PAYMENT SECURITY

11.1 Prior to the COD, the Consumer shall deposit with the Company an interest-free refundable security deposit equal to **three (3) months' estimated Tariff invoice** (the **"Security Deposit"**). The Security Deposit may be applied by the Company against unpaid amounts, and shall be refunded to the Consumer within thirty (30) days after termination of this Agreement, after adjusting any outstanding dues.

11.2 The Consumer shall execute and maintain throughout the Term a NACH debit mandate in favour of the Company in the form set out in Schedule 5.

11.3 If the Consumer fails to pay any undisputed invoice by the date falling thirty (30) days after the Due Date, the Company shall be entitled, on seven (7) days' prior written notice, to electrically isolate the System from the Consumer's internal network. During such disconnection the Consumer shall draw electricity solely from the TSSPDCL grid, and no solar energy shall be supplied or billed. Upon payment in full of all outstanding amounts together with interest and a reconnection fee of Rs. **[___]**, the Company shall restore supply within forty-eight (48) hours. The exercise of this right shall be without prejudice to the Company's other remedies under this Agreement, including termination under Clause 18.

## 16. FORCE MAJEURE

16.1 A **"Force Majeure Event"** means any event or circumstance beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party which prevents or materially hinders performance of its obligations under this Agreement, including acts of God, earthquake, flood, cyclone, fire (not caused by the affected Party), war, riot, civil commotion, industry-wide strikes, epidemic or pandemic, acts of any Governmental Authority, and grid outages exceeding seventy-two (72) continuous hours not attributable to the affected Party.

16.2 The affected Party shall notify the other Party in writing as soon as reasonably practicable of the occurrence of a Force Majeure Event and its expected duration. The obligations of the affected Party (other than obligations to pay amounts already accrued) shall be suspended for the duration of the Force Majeure Event, and the Term shall be extended by an equivalent period.

16.3 If a Force Majeure Event continues for more than one hundred and eighty (180) continuous days, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice, with settlement in accordance with Clause 18.

17.2 If the Parties fail to agree on such adjustment within sixty (60) days of the request, the matter shall be referred to dispute resolution in accordance with Clause 23.

## 18. DEFAULT AND TERMINATION

18.1 Each of the following shall constitute a **"Consumer Default"**:

(a) failure to pay any undisputed amount within thirty (30) days after the Due Date;

(b) failure to maintain the Net Metering Agreement or the Security Deposit;

(c) breach of Clauses 3, 4 or 8.4;

(d) any act, omission or direction of the Consumer that causes physical damage to the System (excluding fair wear and tear); or

(e) insolvency, winding up or dissolution of the Consumer.

18.2 Each of the following shall constitute a **"Company Default"**:

(a) failure to achieve COD within one hundred and eighty (180) days of the Scheduled COD, other than by reason of Force Majeure or Consumer Default;

(b) abandonment of operation of the System for more than sixty (60) continuous days, other than by reason of Force Majeure, Consumer Default or grid unavailability;

(c) persistent failure (three or more occurrences in any twelve-month period) to meet the response times in Clause 7.2; or

(d) insolvency, winding up or dissolution of the Company.

18.3 On the occurrence of a Default, the non-defaulting Party shall give the defaulting Party written notice specifying the Default. The defaulting Party shall have thirty (30) days (or, in the case of a payment Default, fifteen (15) days) from such notice to cure the Default.

18.4 If the Default is not cured within the applicable cure period, the non-defaulting Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice with immediate effect.

18.5 On termination for a Consumer Default, the Consumer shall pay the Company, as a genuine pre-estimate of loss and not as a penalty, a termination payment equal to:

(a) all accrued and unpaid amounts together with interest thereon; and

(b) the lower of (i) the present value of expected Tariff payments for the balance of the Initial Term, discounted at 10% per annum and net of avoided operating costs, or (ii) 1.3 times the depreciated book value of the System (straight-line over twenty (20) years, zero salvage); and

(c) the cost of removal of the System and restoration of the Licensed Area, unless the Consumer exercises the option in Clause 18.6.

18.6 On termination for a Consumer Default, the Consumer shall have the option, exercisable by written notice within thirty (30) days of termination, to purchase the System from the Company at a price equal to 1.3 times its depreciated book value calculated as aforesaid. Payment shall be made within sixty (60) days of exercise, whereupon title shall pass free of encumbrances and the amount payable under Clause 18.5(b) shall not apply in addition to such purchase price.

18.7 On termination for a Company Default, the Company shall, at the election of the Consumer, either (a) remove the System and restore the Licensed Area at its own cost within one hundred and twenty (120) days; or (b) transfer the System to the Consumer for zero consideration, free of encumbrances, together with the benefit of any subsisting manufacturer warranties. The Security Deposit and any amounts pre-paid shall be refunded by the Company.

18.8 The Consumer may, at any time after the seventh (7th) anniversary of the COD and on not less than one hundred and eighty (180) days' written notice, elect to purchase the System from the Company at a price equal to 1.3 times its then depreciated book value. On completion of such purchase, this Agreement shall terminate without further liability, and Clause 19 shall apply in respect of post-termination arrangements.
