# Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) — Bright Roof Power Systems

This folder contains the PPA documents used by **M/s. Bright Roof Power Systems** to sell rooftop-solar generated electricity to residential apartment societies (and, later, commercial clients) under a long-term RESCO / OPEX arrangement.

> **⚠️ Draft — Not Legal Advice.** Everything in this folder is work-in-progress and **must be reviewed by a qualified solar-energy / commercial contracts lawyer in Telangana before signing with any customer.** The documents use standard-form clauses derived from MNRE / SECI / IREDA public templates, adapted for our specific business model.

## Our model in one line

> Bright Roof installs and owns the solar system on the society's rooftop. The society pays a flat Rs/kWh rate for solar units consumed (below grid tariff). The system stays ours for the full term — we do **not** hand over post-breakeven.

This is the key commercial stance that must show up consistently across:
- Marketing
- Sales pitch
- Term sheet / MoU
- The PPA itself
